# Priority Shortlist — scored by difficulty and traffic potential

> **Updated:** now covers all **112** enriched keywords (was 48). The second batch surfaced several opportunities that outrank the original top 10 — table below is the complete ranking.

The audit and gap list ranked by **volume**. This page ranks by what actually matters: **traffic potential ÷ difficulty**. Traffic potential (TP) is the total traffic the #1 page earns from *all* keywords it ranks for — not just the one term — so it captures the real prize. Score = TP ÷ (KD + 1).

This reshuffles the priorities significantly. Several 20k-volume terms are near-unwinnable; several modest-volume terms are worth more than their volume suggests.

### What changed vs. the volume-ranked view

**`grav labs` is the single best opportunity on the list.** Volume 2,700, KD **1**, but traffic potential **49,000** — the page that wins it ranks for the entire Grav brand universe. Its parent topic is "bongs". You'd be building a brand hub, and the term is essentially uncontested. Same story for **`king palms wraps`** (vol 1,000 → TP 15,000, KD 1) and **`e rig`** (vol 4,200 → TP 13,000, KD 0).

**Brand terms dominate the top of this list** — grav labs, king palms, puffco peak pro (TP 22,000, KD 2), hamilton devices (TP 6,800, KD 0), lookah dragon egg (TP 5,000, KD 0). You already stock several. In a category where you can't run ads, authorised-stockist brand hubs are the cheapest qualified traffic available.

**`nectar collector` deserves top billing among your existing pages**: 28,000 volume, TP 18,000, **KD 0**, and you already have `/collections/nectar-collectors-straws` with 29 products and 6,912 characters. A KD 0 keyword where you own a strong page and don't rank top-10 is almost always an internal-linking failure. Highest-certainty win on the site.

**`dab rig`** — 50,000 volume, TP 31,000, **KD 3**. I'd assumed the biggest term would be the hardest. It isn't.

**Pattern worth naming:** nearly every top-scoring term is **KD 0–3 with traffic potential 5–30× its own volume**, and most map to a collection you already have. This is not a market you need to fight your way into — it's one where the pages exist and nothing points at them.

## Revised order of work

1. **`cannabis vaporizer` + `dry herb vaporizer`** — KD 0, TP 35,000, and `/collections/dry-herb-vaporizers` shows only **5 products**. Merchandise it, then link to it.
2. **`dab rig`** — KD 3, 31,000 TP, page exists with 28 products. Same treatment.
3. **Brand hubs** — grav (TP 65,000 KD 2), puffco (TP 43,000 KD 2), pulsar (three KD-0 terms), king palms, hamilton devices, lookah. All have collections or near-collections already; all have **zero blog links**. Combined TP well over 150,000.
4. **`e rig` / `e nail` / `dab banger`** — KD 0, combined TP ~22,000, all adjacent to collections you already have. Likely naming fixes rather than new builds.
5. **`terp slurper`, `ash catcher`, `cute bongs`, `beaker bong`** — KD 0–1, TP 3,800–5,900 each.
6. Only then: the high-KD volume terms.

Note the CPC column in the source data is near-zero across the board — that's the ad restriction showing up, not low commercial value. Ignore CPC as a value signal in this niche.
