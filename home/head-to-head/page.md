# Head-to-head: the keywords your catalogue already deserves

Cross-referencing the ranking keywords of the two largest competitors in your set against your **actual 3,909-product Shopify catalogue** and your 122 collections. Ahrefs, United States, April 2026.

**Method:** pulled every keyword Smoke Cartel and DankGeek rank top-20 for at 100+ monthly volume (1,000 each), stripped branded / single-word / "near me" / "amazon" / "reddit" terms, removed anything you already rank top-10 for, then kept only keywords where **your own product catalogue contains matching inventory**. Each survivor was mapped back to the collection page that should own it.

**Full data: [smokerolla-headtohead.csv](https://work.leopoldo-s-space.letaido.app/downloads/smokerolla-headtohead.csv)** — 773 keywords with volume, difficulty, competitor traffic, matched collection, and how many of your products match.

## Caveats

- Competitor traffic is what *they* earn today, not what you would earn. Treat it as relative sizing between opportunities, not a forecast.
- Keyword-to-product matching is token-based against product handles; the `catalogue_matches` column in the CSV is an approximation, and a low count should be sanity-checked in Shopify before you build a page on it.
- Keyword sets are capped at the top 1,000 per competitor by traffic, so very-long-tail overlap is under-counted.
