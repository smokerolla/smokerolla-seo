# Smokerolla DTC SEO

Organic search work for the **direct-to-consumer** side of [smokerolla.com](https://smokerolla.com) (Shopify). The wholesale / dropship motion is out of scope here.

**Market:** United States · **Data sources:** Ahrefs (US) + first-party crawl of all 122 collections, 28 pages and the sitemap index.

**Baseline:** 1,679 monthly organic visits · 444 keywords · 3,909 products · 0 paid traffic (restricted category).

## The one-sentence version

The content engine works and the collection pages are well-built, but authority never reaches the store: blog posts outrank the store's own collections, 50 of 122 collections receive zero internal links from the blog, and those collections have a median of **zero referring domains**. The fix is redistributing internal links, merchandising and de-duplication — not more content.
